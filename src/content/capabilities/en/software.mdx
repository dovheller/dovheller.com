I build static and hybrid websites with Astro and Next.js. I use code splitting to keep initial loads lean and give content crawlable URLs.

I build native Android apps in Kotlin with Gradle, including apps for field data collection and operations.

In field systems, I combine GPS, audio capture, transcription, and AI-assisted analysis. I use coding agents in development and review the resulting changes against the specification.

I also build online stores and direct ordering systems.
