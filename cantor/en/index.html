CANTORIAL ARCHIVE · 2000–2018

Cantor Dov Heller

Eighteen years before the ark and on concert stages in Israel and abroad. Dramatic baritone, student of Cantor Moshe Stern.

The story

I grew up in a Hasidic, musical home in Jerusalem, and after my wedding I opened a computer store. In 2000 a customer walked in: Cantor Moshe Stern. He heard the voice, taught me the High Holiday services for a post in Stuttgart, and wrote pieces I premiered.

After a concert with Stern and Cantor Ben Zion Miller at Beth El in New York, Miller recommended me to Shomrim Laboker in Montreal, where I served as head cantor for about three years. Back in Israel from 2005: High Holidays in Migdal HaEmek, many Shabbatot at the Jerusalem Great Synagogue, and stages in Moscow and St. Petersburg.

At 40 I chose one world and went back to technology. Today I lead HDIGITAL. I took the listening with me.
